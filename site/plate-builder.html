5 · Export

“Risk + clean” downloads two PNGs: one with risk fill, one without. Live streams: plates are full-screen and opaque. OBS: add the 1920 × 1080 PNG as an Image source at the bottom of the scene. StreamYard: export 1280 × 720 and upload it under Brand → Backgrounds (as an overlay it would cover the cameras).
